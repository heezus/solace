# Glossary

Every canonical name and term, alphabetical. Spell it here once; spell it this way everywhere.

| Term | Type | Definition | Source section |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bloom, the | Faction | A living, alien growth that consumes worlds. It hunted the Lumen to Solace. Future third adversary; the Life force of the triad. | World & Lore |
| Bronze Dawn | Tech node / era gate | Research that ends the stone age and opens the next era. | First Playable |
| Kith, the | Faction | The player's people, starting in the stone age. Working name. | World & Lore |
| Lumen, the | Faction | Magic-wielding refugees who arrived on a fallen star, fleeing a greater enemy. Rivals to the player; can be traded with, fought, or bridged. | World & Lore |
| Solace | Place; game title | The planet where the game takes place, and the title of the game. | World & Lore |
| Star shard | Item | A faintly glowing stone from the ancient star fall; the first, sparing trace of magic. | World & Lore |
| Starfallen, the | Faction (exonym) | What the player's people call the Lumen. Used before you learn their real name. | World & Lore |

## Kith jobs (2026-09-30)
The people are the **Kith**. Each working Kith is named for its job:

| Job | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Woodcutter | Gatherer's Hut (Wood) | Fells trees |
| Quarrier | Gatherer's Hut (Stone) | Breaks rock |
| Knapper | Gatherer's Hut (Flint) | Gathers flint from the riverbed gravel |
| Forager | Gatherer's Hut (Berries) | Picks berries |
| Thatcher | Gatherer's Hut (Fiber) | Cuts flax for fiber |
| Reaper | Gatherer's Hut (wild Grain), Field | Cuts grain |
| Digger | Gatherer's Hut (Clay) | Digs clay |
| Fisher | Fishing Weir | Tends the weir |
| Collier | Charcoal Pit | Burns charcoal |
| Roper | Twine Post | Twists rope |
| Potter | Kiln | Fires brick |
| Miller | Grindstone | Grinds flour |
| Hauler | Paths & Haulers | Carries goods between buildings |
| Idle | anywhere | Waiting for a job |

A hut's worker takes the title of whatever that hut gathers most.

## Flax (2026-09-30)
**Flax** is the source of Fiber. Wild flax grows in clustered patches on open grassland and meadows, and isn't tied to water. A Kith learns it by hand-harvesting like any other resource, and a Gatherer's Hut's Thatcher cuts it. Bare grass yields nothing. Every map places a flax patch within reach of the Hearth. Flax can also be sown: once Cordage is known, a Flax Field (2 Fiber a tile, dragged on open grassland) grows more of it, cut by a Gatherer's Hut or by hand as wild flax is (decided by Jon, 2026-10-03). (It was Reeds for a few hours on 2026-09-30, and it was renamed when Jon dropped the water requirement.)

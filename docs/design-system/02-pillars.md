# Pillars

The rules every feature serves. If a feature serves none of them, it gets cut. Written by the director thread on 2026-10-21 from decisions Jon already made; Jon approved the plan they belong to (review: https://claude.ai/artifact/MjCEMLkSPUJ6G9HYbmuW8C).

| # | Pillar | What it means in play | What it rules out |
|---|--------|-----------------------|-------------------|
| 1 | What the Kith know is the progress | Each era teaches one new way of knowing. A reset keeps knowledge, not stock. | Eras that are only bigger numbers; a second new system in the same era. |
| 2 | Build the machine, then watch it run | Automation is earned (hands, huts, roads, rules) and every part of it is visible: who carries what, where it waits. | Capacity that hides; a stock number that says "fine" while a machine starves. |
| 3 | Every threat has a clock and an answer | Pressure shows its timer, and a sensible player can afford some answer at the moment it arrives. Pressure tests the machine; it never ambushes it. | A threat whose only counter is tiers away; a clock that ignores what the player does. |
| 4 | Small numbers that mean something | Prices in made goods, stocks in the hundreds. No single good can wall an era for good. | Piles of thousands; a finite good with no slower substitute. |
| 5 | Hopeful, then ominous | The tone darkens as the Lumen and the Bloom arrive; each reset returns to hope. | Dread before Starfall; a cheerful Livewire. |

Every era contract ([30](30-era-contracts.md)) and every pass bar traces back to one of these.

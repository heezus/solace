# Mechanics

## Core loop
What the player does every 30 seconds, every 10 minutes, every session.

**Decided (2026-09-29):** automation at the core. Three hooks, all wanted:
1. **Design:** a complicated, interdependent tech tree where resources and production chains feed each other.
2. **Watch:** the machine runs on its own and numbers climb (idle feel).
3. **Progress:** clear stages; reaching the next one changes what's possible.

**Two trees (2026-09-29):** a tech tree and a magic tree. Discovering the second one midway, then bridging the two, is a major stage that reshapes production.

**Eras (2026-09-29):** the tech tree climbs through eras, Civ-style, from simple beginnings to super advanced. Each era is a stage that changes what the factory can make. **Start in the stone age.** Magic shows up only as sparing traces (odd stones, strange readings) until a major discovery opens the magic tree. The discovery is the refugee ship crashing mid-game; the early traces are ancient star shards (see World & Lore).

## Progression
**Prestige / reset (planned, 2026-09-29):** the game will have a prestige loop where you reset progress for lasting bonuses. 

Three layers, picked by how far you got and how much magic you used (ordering proposed, 2026-09-29):

| Layer | Story | Trigger (draft) | Carries over (draft) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Exodus | Pack up and resettle a new region | Low-magic runs; the region is exhausted; draft: keeping the refugees at arm's length | Knowledge: faster research, starting blueprints |
| 2. Cataclysm | Magic overreach wipes out the civilization | Heavy magic use; draft: taking magic by force | Relics: magic artifacts found in the ruins |
| 3. Time loop | The planet rewinds; only you remember | Deepest layer, late game; draft: reached by fully bridging with the refugees | Memory: permanent meta-upgrades |

**Refugee choice (2026-09-29):** how the player treats the star refugees (trade, conflict or a mix) steers which prestige layer they reach. The mapping in the table above is a draft.

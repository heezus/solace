# Era 4: Ironfall

Status: **stages 1, 2 and 3 built** (2026-10-07, 2026-10-08, 2026-10-08): coal and iron, the south growth, Coal Seams, Ironstone, Bloomery, Iron Tools, the fourth tab and the era's goals, then Teardown, parts and Lessons, then steam and the end (Boiler, Rails and the Steam Cart, Forge and Steel, the Shard Lamp and Shard Boiler, Bloom Sampling, the Beast Pen, Taught Hands II, the Iron Plough, Livewire and its end card), all on placeholder art. The second map growth (west) is deferred. Stage 3's costs and times were tuned with the Ironfall pacing bot on 2026-10-09 (table below); every other number is still a placeholder to tune, and anything marked *default* is a pick Claude made that the project owner can change. Mechanics page, so Claude owns it. It follows [18](18-roadmap.md) (the shape and the Decided list) and picks up where [Starfall](16-starfall.md) ends.

## The feeling
- Bronze Dawn was metal and distance. Starfall was strangers and meaning. **Ironfall is machines and borrowed knowledge.** The Kith finally have the strength to build engines, and the Lumen have things the Kith cannot make yet. The era is about **opening things up to see how they work**.
- Warm and busy, with a thread of dread. Smoke, rails and lamps by day. At the edge of the fog, three patches of something green that do nothing yet.
- **Length target:** about 20 minutes. It starts on the Starfall ending card and ends on a short card of its own.

## One new thing: Teardown
The era adds exactly one mechanic ([12](12-knowledge-is-progress.md): each era adds one new way of knowing). Iron, coal and steam are the content around it, not a second system.

### Rules
- **Finds are parts.** A part is a whole object, not a pile: a Lumen lamp core, a hull gear from the Wreck, a Bloom sample. Parts come from expedition finds, from the Lumen Camp (what they will hand over, see **Trust** below) and from the three Bloom patches.
- Build a **Teardown Bench** (iron and brick, near the Hearth). Haulers carry parts to it like any input, and the Bench picks the next part from a short queue on its own. One part takes about 40 seconds.
- Taking a part apart **consumes it and teaches one Lesson.** A Lesson is permanent and small: a recipe, a new use for a shard, a building, a boost. The part is the key, and the Lesson is what the Kith keep.
- **Lessons are a short, finite list** (about 8 in the era). Each part names the Lesson it holds, shown on the part's card before you open it, so the player chooses what to learn first. No grinding: a second copy of a part gives scrap (a little Iron), never a second Lesson.
- A **Lessons list** sits on the Lore tab beside the Glyph Wall. It shows each Lesson as locked (the part is not found yet), found (a part is on the way) or learned (what it gave).
- Why it fits the pillar: it is "what the Kith know" made literal. The tech tree says what the Kith *can* learn; Teardown is the moment they *do*. Expeditions already bring the finds home, so Teardown reuses a loop the player owns instead of adding a chore.

### The Lessons (the finite list)
| Lesson | From | Teaches |
|---|---|---|
| Lamp core | Lumen Camp or the Wreck | **Shard Lamp**: a lamp post that burns one shard a long while and lights 3 tiles |
| Heat plate | the Wreck | **Shard Boiler**: a boiler that burns shards, so far less coal |
| Hull gear | the Wreck | **Iron Gears**: a good that speeds every machine it touches (Speed +25% in a workshop it is fed to) |
| Seed pod | Lumen Camp | **Starfruit** ripens in coal smoke too (the Starfall crop, a second home) |
| Water glass | Lumen Camp | **Rain Barrel**: Fields near one never wilt in a dry spell (placeholder effect, +20% Field yield) |
| Spore | Bloom patch | the Kith learn what the Bloom eats: sign patches show their reach on the map |
| Root | Bloom patch | the Kith learn how it spreads: the Bloom's slowest ground is lit on the map |
| Sap | Bloom patch | the Kith learn what it fears: **Lantern Parties**, expeditions that may stay out past dusk |

The three Bloom Lessons are the three **sample types**, one echo each for the carry-over ([18](18-roadmap.md)); the three Lumen lessons held by trust are the first echoes the Lumen offer.

## The world
### New map content
- **Coal seams** sit on the flanks of the rock ridge, near the old copper hills but on the near side. **Iron hills** lie in the east strip behind the copper, a longer road than copper was. Both are ore tiles, mined by a Mine like copper and tin.
- **The map grows once more,** to the south (the east grew in Bronze Dawn). The new land starts under fog like the last. It holds the rest of the iron and the three Bloom patches. *Default:* the growth comes with Ironstone, so the first iron is not a long walk.
- **Three Bloom patches** stand at the far edge of the fog, each a small spread of green and magenta ground a few tiles wide, with a sample on it. They do nothing (page 18: signs only). An expedition can take a sample from each. A fourth, large patch near the Wreck is already drawn by Starfall stage 3 (the first Bloom sign); the three here are what the signs lead to.
- **Coal is finite on purpose.** Each seam holds a pile and runs out (about 500 each, three seams). Iron is plentiful. This is the era's pressure: coal runs the boilers and the furnaces, so the player wants a second fuel, and the Lumen's shard boiler is the way out. It also sets up Livewire, where fire is the Bloom's enemy and fuel matters.

### New items
- **Coal** (fuel, better than Charcoal), **Iron Ore**, **Iron**, **Steel**, **Iron Gears** (a Lesson, not a stockpile good).
- **Parts:** Lamp Core, Heat Plate, Hull Gear, Seed Pod, Water Glass, and the three samples (Spore, Root, Sap). Parts are rare one-offs: they sit in the pack and the Bench, never the main stockpile counters.
- The Kith eat what they ate before. Nothing new is food in this era except Starfruit, already an echo of Starfall.

### New buildings
| Building | Tab | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Coal Mine (a Mine on a coal tile) | Metal | Digs Coal, two Kith as with any Mine |
| Bloomery | Metal | 2 Iron Ore + 1 Coal becomes Iron, slow |
| Forge | Metal | Iron + Coal becomes Steel; needs the Blast Furnace tech |
| Boiler | Workshops | Burns Coal and powers machines in 5 tiles, **no river needed** (the Water Wheel's limit is gone) |
| Rail | Logistics | Rail tier on the road ladder (path, gravel, paved, **rail**): 8x open ground; the Steam Cart runs only on rails and carries 40 |
| Teardown Bench | Lore | See above |
| Shard Lamp | Lore | Lit by a Lesson; lights 3 tiles, gives expeditions leave to stay out past dusk once Sap is learned |
| Beast Pen | Land | Beasts and the beast cart (see **Beasts**) |

## The tech tree (Era 4): 16 techs
The same five lanes and rules as [09](09-tech-tree.md): almost every tech past the roots joins two branches, there are side branches, and a test checks both. It unlocks on the **Starfall ending card** and draws as a fourth tab (the magic tree stays a set of gifts, not a tab, until it is built). Costs sit in the same tier bands and are paid in the previous tier's goods. Lore-lane cards are gated by Lessons where it reads well, never by a plain requirement (the Star Lore rule).

| 4 | Blast Furnace | Hearth | Bloomery, Boiler | Forge; Iron x2 per firing |

The gate costs 50 Steel, 70 Iron, 60 Brick and 40 Rope, and the three Bloom Lessons (tuned 2026-10-09; it began as 100 Steel and 100 Iron).

## Trust and the Lumen lean
- **Trust decides how freely the Lumen hand things over** (page 18). *Default bands:* low trust, the Lumen lend no parts, and the only way to a Lumen Lesson is an expedition to the Wreck. Middle trust, the Lumen Camp offers two parts (Lamp core, Seed pod). High trust, it offers all of its parts and the Heat plate is shared.
- **Allies** (high lean) hand over every Lumen part; **neighbours** (middle) hand over two; **enemies** (low) hand over none and the player digs the Wreck for the rest. The Wreck always holds three parts, so no run is locked out of the Boiler or the Lamp.
- Whatever the lean, the three Bloom Lessons come from the Bloom patches, so the Kith are never wholly dependent on the Lumen. The lean is one more input to the three resets, as page 18 says.

## Beasts
- **Default:** one side branch, the Beast Pen, in this era. A **Beast Pen** tames one shard-touched beast (a bluish animal that glows faintly in the dark). A **beast cart** pulls 30 on roads, needs no coal, and cannot use rails.
- It rides on Starfall (the starstuff touched the beasts) and costs the player a short chain, not a system. The art slots are already logged in [17](17-needs-and-upgrades.md).

## Ending
- Researching **Livewire** plays a short end card. The lamps burn without a flame, the Kith hear a hum down the new wires, and, far to the north, something green moves in the fog.
- The game keeps running so the player can keep building, as Bronze Dawn and Starfall do. Livewire (era 5) is the next design page.

## What stage 1 built, and where it differs (2026-10-07)
- **Opening.** The era's techs wait for the story: Ironfall begins the tick after the Starfall ending card is put away (`ironfall_begun`), and the four stage-1 techs, the roots Coal Seams and Ironstone first, come into view then. The fourth tab opens at the same moment and is where the research board opens from then on. The Goals panel switches to a nine-goal Ironfall list.
- **The south.** The land grows 22 rows south when **either** Coal Seams or Ironstone is learned (page 19 said Ironstone; either keeps coal from being stranded when the player learns it first). It is made from the map's seed like the east, checked for fairness over many seeds (three separate seams on the near side, plenty of iron and some of it near, an open way south), and starts under fog. Coal and iron are placed there only, so the stone age and Bronze Dawn maps, and the golden pacing runs, do not change.
- **Finite coal.** Each of the three seams holds 500 shared by its tiles. A Coal Mine or a hand dig draws from the pile; when it is gone the seam turns to a Spent Seam and a Mine on it says it has nothing left. Iron is plentiful. Both ores need their tech to be named, to be dug by hand and to be mined (`gated` tiles).
- **Metal.** A Coal Mine (Metal tab) digs coal; the plain Mine also stands on Iron Hills once Ironstone is learned. The Bloomery turns 2 Iron Ore and 1 Coal into Iron. Iron Tools (Iron and Wood, at the Tool Bench or by hand) give a worker Speed 2.75 against bronze's 2.0 and last 300 jobs, and workers take them before bronze.
- **Not yet** (built in stage 3, below). The Boiler, Rails, Forge, Steel, Beast Pen, the Shard Boiler, Bloom Sampling, the gate and its end card: their techs were on the board, locked, and carry `stage` 3. Techs of the era are defined in `scripts/data/ironfall/techs.gd`, one constant each, since a constant cannot merge dictionaries.

## What stage 2 built, and where it differs (2026-10-08)
- **Teardown.** The tech opens on the board and the Bench (Lore tab, iron 20 and brick 40, one per town, within the Hearth's reach, no worker). Haulers carry parts from the pack to the Bench like goods (a click on a part in the Bench panel moves it to the front, or a button carries it by hand), the Bench holds three, and the front one takes 40 s. That consumes the part and teaches its Lesson for good. A copy of a Lesson already learned is stripped for 6 Iron in 8 s. Demolish the Bench and its parts go back to the pack. Code: `scripts/teardown.gd` (the block), `scripts/teardown_finds.gd` (sources), `scripts/lessons.gd`, `scripts/lessons_list.gd`, `scripts/teardown_panel.gd`; words and numbers in `scripts/data/teardown.gd`.
- **Sources.** The Wreck holds three parts (Heat plate, Lamp core, Hull gear, in that order unless the town holds or knows one): a light or standard trip brings one, a heavy pack two, a late party half. The Lumen Camp hands over a part every 45 s by the lean the Starfall ended on (`starfall.lean`): enemies none, neighbours Lamp core and Seed pod, allies those and Water glass and Heat plate. Three Bloom patches (spore, root, sap) lie in the last 8 rows of the south land, at least 5 tiles wide, under fog, and are a fourth target at the Expedition Post; a trip takes the sample and leaves plain Bloom ground. The map and fairness code is `scripts/map_south.gd`; the stone age and Bronze maps are unchanged.
- **Placeholder flag.** Bloom Sampling is a stage-3 tech, so `Data.BLOOM_SAMPLING_PLACEHOLDER` (true) lets stage 2 take samples. Stage 3 set it false and the tech decides.
- **The eight Lessons.** Live: Hull gear (Iron Gears, a workshop input that adds 25% speed, fitted by haulers), Water glass (Rain Barrel, +20% Field yield within 3 tiles), Seed pod (Starfruit's second home: +50% berries near coal smoke), Spore (a ring where the Bloom eats) and Root (roads and rocks lit). Recorded in stage 2 and live since stage 3: Lamp core (Shard Lamp), Heat plate (Shard Boiler), Sap (Lantern Parties). Iron Gears are not a top-bar counter.
- **Where it differs.** The Bloom patches are in the south land rather than the west (the west land does not exist before Rails). The Lessons list sits under the Glyph Wall's marks and at the Bench rather than in a tab of its own. Three goals join the Ironfall list (Teardown, a Bench, a first Lesson), and its closing line now points at stage 3.
- **Saves and tests.** A `teardown` entry under `game`, optional with defaults, so saves stay VERSION 1 and the frozen pre-Ironfall fixture loads unchanged. `tests/teardown_tests.gd` covers the data, Bench, haulers, sources, patches, Lessons, panels and the save; it runs in the full suite and in `-- ironfall`.

## What stage 3 built, and where it differs (2026-10-08)
- **Fed buildings.** The Boiler, the Shard Boiler, the Shard Lamp and the Beast Pen have no worker. Haulers keep each stocked with what it burns (a small `stock`: 6 Coal, 4 Shards, 2 Shards, 4 Grain) and it burns that on its own (`scripts/steam.gd`; a `fed` building in `scripts/data/buildings.gd`). The numbers and words are in `scripts/data/steam.gd`.
- **Boiler** (Workshops tab: stone 30, brick 50, iron 10; tech cost brick 100, coal 60, bronze 20). It powers machines within 5 tiles, no river needed. It lights when a machine in reach has a worker, its inputs and room for the goods, burns 1 Coal for 20 s while lit, and stays banked when nobody wants power, so a Boiler left alone does not eat the finite coal. With no coal in it the machine says "No power" and the Boiler asks for Coal. The Water Wheel still works as before. **Shard Boiler** (stone 30, brick 50, iron 20, bronze 10): the same, burning 1 Shard for 80 s and no coal; it needs the Heat plate Lesson and the Craft gift.
- **Forge** (Metal tab: stone 40, brick 60, iron 20; tech Blast Furnace). Needs power: 2 Iron and 1 Coal make 1 Steel in 8 s (a Smith). The Blast Furnace tech also gives the Bloomery a second Iron a firing (an Output bonus on `bloomery`), which is the "Iron x2 per firing" of the page.
- **Steel Tools.** 1 Steel and 2 Wood at the Tool Bench or by hand; Speed +225% (3.25 against iron's 2.75, so another 50 points) and 600 jobs; workers take them first. The top bar's Tools readout counts them.
- **Rail** (Logistics tab: wood 2 and iron 1 a tile; tech Rails). The fourth road tier, speed 4.0, which is 8 times open ground. It lays over a path, gravel or paved road for the difference in cost and stops at the river. Because Rail is the top of `ROAD_SPEEDS`, the Stone Bridge and the lifting of old saves now name `Data.PAVED_TIER` rather than "the last tier". Placeholder art draws it as the paved road with two rails and sleepers laid over it by code.
- **Steam Cart.** A **Steam Shed** (Logistics tab: wood 60, iron 40, brick 30, with the Rails tech) turns one hauler into a Steam Cart, as a Cart Shed does a hand cart; it needs one Rail tile in the town, or it makes no cart. Carts go to haulers in the order steam, hand, beast. A Steam Cart with steam up carries 40 (4 times a hauler's load, Carrying Poles doubling it) and runs **only on Rail**. It burns 1 Coal for every 3 trips. With no Coal, or with no work reachable on Rail, it hauls like a plain hauler (10, on any road). So a town can lay Rail to the busy places and leave the rest on roads. Rail is part of the road ladder, so any hauler and any hand cart can use it too.
- **Beast Pen** (Gathering tab: wood 60, grain 30, rope 20; tech Beast Pen). Haulers bring it Grain; it eats 1 every 6 s for 60 s of taming (10 Grain), and then a tamed beast pulls a **beast cart**: 30 a trip on roads, no coal, never on Rail. One cart for each tamed Pen. A tamed Pen asks for nothing more.
- **Shard Lamp and the Shard.** Once Shard Lamps is learned, a hold on the Strange Stone chips off 1 Shard in 5 s (a click still finds it the first time). The **Shard Lamp** (Lore tab: iron 6, brick 8, stone 6; tech Shard Lamps and the Lamp core Lesson) burns 1 Shard for 300 s and lights 3 tiles (drawn as a pale glow). **Lantern Parties:** with the Sap Lesson learned and a Shard Lamp burning, an expedition has 1.5 times the day (120 s rather than 80 s) before dusk. The Expedition Post shows the longer day while it holds.
- **Gifts and Lessons gate techs.** A tech with a `gift` waits for that Starfall gift to be read (Shard Lamps for Shardlight, Shard Boiler for Craft), and the gate's `lessons` are Lessons that must be learned. Both count as open requirements on the board, the tech panel and the suggestion line; this is `Research.open_needs`. A building's `lesson` still waits for the Bench as in stage 2.
- **Bloom Sampling** is a real tech now: with the placeholder flag off, an expedition needs it to take a sample.
- **Taught Hands II** (side branch). Teach-by-doing takes half as many harvests (rounded up), and when a Kith learns a resource, every other resource harvested by hand at least half that many times is learned with it. The people know a resource all together, so "every hut of that kind" is read as this: a job learned brings along the ones you were already part way to.
- **Iron Plough** (side branch). Fields yield another 50% of the bundle, on top of the Plough's and the Ploughshare's.
- **Livewire** (the gate). Cost 50 Steel, 70 Iron, 60 Brick and 40 Rope, and the three Bloom Lessons learned (Spore, Root and Sap). Researching it records `livewire_lit`, pauses the game behind an end card (The Wires Hum; "Keep building"), and puts the run in the profile as the Falling Star does. The game then goes on. The Goals list closes on "The age of iron is done. The game goes on."
- **Goals.** Nine more join the Ironfall list (21 in all): a Boiler, Rail laid, a Steam Shed, a Forge, the first Steel, Steel, a Shard Lamp, Bloom Sampling and Livewire.
- **Where it differs.** (1) The gate asks for **the three Bloom Lessons** rather than "one of each Bloom sample": a sample is a part, and opening it at the Bench consumes it, so the Lesson is what the player can still show. (2) **The second (west) map growth is deferred.** The map is a grid indexed from its top-left, so growing west shifts every coordinate in every system and every save; the rails fit in the land the town already has, and the Bloom patches are in the south (stage 2). The default below stands for a later stage. (3) The Boiler lights only for a machine that wants power, so it is a fire to feed, not a tax. (4) Rail has no art of its own and Steel Tools and the Shard borrow icons; the slots are in `docs/art/requests.md`.
- **Saves and tests.** Optional fields only, with defaults: a building's `burn`, and a Kith's `cart_kind`, `fire` and `hot`. A cart in an older save is a hand cart. Saves stay VERSION 1 and the frozen pre-Ironfall fixture loads unchanged. `tests/steam_tests.gd` covers the data, Rail, the Boilers, the Forge, the Lamp, the carts, the Pen, Steel, the fields, Taught Hands II, the Shard, the gates, the Lantern Party, Livewire and the saves; it runs in the full suite, in `-- ironfall` and alone with `-- steam`. The `ironfall_steam` start in the dev menu opens on a Boiler, a Forge and a Steam Shed.

## Stage 3 pacing (tuned 2026-10-09)
`tests/autoplay_ironfall.gd` plays Ironfall from a stage start to the Livewire gate through the public `Sim` calls only (it extends the Bronze bot): it builds the Coal and Iron Mines (and moves on when a seam runs dry), the Bloomeries, a Forge beside the Boiler, Rail for the Steam Cart, and sends the Bloom sample trips first because they are the long pole. `tests/tools/pace.gd N MIN ironfall [steam|teardown] [cache]` runs it on map N and prints the clock at each milestone. The aim was seven to nine minutes from `ironfall_steam` to the gate. The untuned numbers took 7.6 to 17.3 minutes on maps 1 to 8 (median 11.8), the iron and steel throughput of the Bloomeries and Forge being the limit, never the Bloom path (done by about 5 minutes).

| Number | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Livewire | 100 Steel, 100 Iron, 60 Brick, 40 Rope | 50 Steel, 70 Iron, 60 Brick, 40 Rope |
| Steel (tech) | iron 80, coal 80, brick 60 | iron 60, coal 60, brick 50 |
| Bloom Sampling (tech) | iron 50, brick 60, rope 60 | iron 40, brick 50, rope 60 |
| Shard Boiler (tech) | iron 60, brick 80, bronze 30 | iron 45, brick 60, bronze 30 |
| Forge (building) | stone 40, brick 80, iron 30; 10 s a firing | stone 40, brick 60, iron 20; 8 s a firing |

With these the bot reaches the gate in 5.2 to 10.3 minutes on maps 1 to 8 (median 7.8). Unchanged on purpose: the Boiler, Rails, Blast Furnace and Shard Lamps techs (the start already has them), the Bloomery (a stage 2 number), the Steam Cart's and Boiler's rates, and the Rail cost. Two things the bot showed: (1) a Forge placed outside the Boiler's 5 tiles never gets power, so `ironfall_steam` now stands the Forge beside the Boiler; (2) haulers are dealt over every Storehouse that has a road-linked building, so a Storehouse on a road of its own, cut off from the Hearth, holds a share of the haulers that can never reach the metal works (the bot tears such a post down; a rule that fixes it for a player is a game-code change for a later PR, since it moves the golden pacing hashes).

## Staging (how it gets built)
Three stages, each its own PR with placeholder art and the art slots logged in `docs/art/requests.md`, so Codex can start in parallel.
1. **Iron.** Coal and iron tiles, the south growth, Coal Seams, Ironstone, Bloomery, Iron Tools, the fourth tab and the items. The era is playable without Teardown.
2. **Teardown.** The Bench, parts, the Lessons list, expedition finds, the Lumen Camp's parts and the Bloom patches. The Lessons turn on the nodes they name.
3. **Steam and the end.** Boiler, Rails, Forge, Steel, the Shard Boiler, Bloom Sampling, the Beast Pen, the gate and the end card. *Built, 2026-10-08.*

# 30. Era contracts

Status: **draft** (2026-10-21, director thread). Each era is specified like a block in a chip: what comes in, what must come out, and the rules that must always hold. Entry ranges are filled from the Phase 0 baseline (5 seeds, every era, earned chain from a fresh stone age). A result counts only when it is measured against the contract.

## Rules that hold in every era
1. **Length:** 15 to 25 game minutes on at least 4 of 5 seeds.
2. **No dead end:** from any start inside the entry range, the gate can be reached without debug keys, gifts or hand-edited state.
3. **No finite wall:** running out of any finite good slows the era; it never stops it (pillar 4).
4. **Answer before threat:** when a pressure first bites (a season, the tide, a famine), at least one answer to it is affordable from a start in range (pillar 3).
5. **Visible logistics:** no workshop starves for more than 60 s while its input is in stock (pillar 2).
6. **One new thing:** the era adds one new way of knowing; everything else is content around it (pillar 1).

| Livewire | Write the rules | Standing orders that the town needs | The Bloom tide, paced by the town | from baseline |
| Skyreach | Read the sky, choose the ending | Survey the sky; hand off to a reset | The Bloom arrives | not designed |

An entry range lists: Kith, idle Kith, haulers, key stocks, techs learned, coal in the ground, paused buildings. A bot start outside its range is a bot bug, not a balance target.

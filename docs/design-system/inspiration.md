## Patterns
1. **Automation and building is the core love.** Factorio, Satisfactory, Timberborn, Oddsparks, Against the Storm, Prison Architect: over 700 hours combined.
2. **Extraction and risk.** Duckov, ZERO Sievert, Gray Zone Warfare, Miscreated: go out, grab loot, get home alive.
3. **Deduction mysteries, all rated very highly.** Obra Dinn, Roottrees are Dead, The Operator, Blue Prince. Short, but he loves them.
4. **Genre blends win.** His praise for DREDGE and Timberborn is specifically about genres overlapping. Cult of the Lamb (cute + dark) fits too.
5. **Gripes to avoid:** slow tech progression between sessions (Against the Storm), unfair difficulty spikes (Cult of the Lamb's last boss).

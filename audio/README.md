# Solace audio

Original sounds by Codex, dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

All files are 44.1 kHz Ogg Vorbis. Effects are mono and target −18 LUFS. Cues shorter than three seconds are measured over the clip duration. Ambience is stereo and targets −28 LUFS.
